DSA Course Topics

UNIT I: Introduction, Arrays & Sorting
Introduction to Data Structures (Definition, Classification, and Operations) Algorithms & Complexity (Time-Space Tradeoff and Analysis) Arrays & Matrices (1D, 2D, Multi-Dimensional, and Sparse Matrices) Array Operations (Traversing, Inserting, Deleting, and Merging) Searching Algorithms (Linear Search vs. Binary Search) Sorting Algorithms (Bubble Sort, Selection Sort, and Insertion Sort)
UNIT II: Linked Lists & Hashing
Introduction to Linked Lists (Concepts and Comparison with Arrays) Singly Linked Lists (Representation and Core Operations) Doubly & Circular Linked Lists (Structure and Implementation) Applications of Linked Lists (Polynomial Addition) Hashing & Hash Tables (Hash Functions and Collision Concepts) Collision Resolution Techniques (Open Addressing and Chaining)
UNIT III: Stacks, Queues & Recursion
Stacks (Array & Linked List Representation, and Core Operations) Applications of Stacks (Polish Notation, Infix to Postfix Conversion, and Evaluation) Recursion (Runtime Stack, Factorial, GCD, Fibonacci, and Towers of Hanoi) Queues (Array & Linked List Representation, and Core Operations) Types of Queues (Simple, Circular, Double-Ended, and Priority Queues) Applications of Queues (Real-world use cases)
UNIT IV: Trees & Graphs
Graphs (Terminology, Representation, and Traversal Techniques) Introduction to Trees (Terminology and Binary Tree Traversals) Binary Search Trees (BST) (Insertion, Deletion, and Searching) Height Balanced Trees (AVL Tree Insertion and Deletion)
